## Current Objective and State

Pocket Playground is the production TryRaisins portfolio. `src/pages/index.astro` imports `src/prototypes/portfolio-redesign/Play.astro`. The page is a ruled notebook with pencil drawings and spinning tops, and it now reads as a physical sketchbook: Caveat handwriting for annotations, a pinned sequence of project prints, and a lighter three.js scene.

- Copy, project data, destinations, the suspended BetPicks treatment, the contact reveal, the XOR-masked email and all SEO/JSON-LD are unchanged. Preserve them.
- Fonts are self-hosted from `src/styles/fonts.css` + `public/fonts/*.woff2` (five families, latin only): Bricolage Grotesque 600, DM Sans 400-600, Caveat 400-700, JetBrains Mono 400, Permanent Marker 400. There is no Google Fonts request. Barlow Condensed and Public Sans were removed; do not reintroduce them.
- The page is a school exercise book: off-white paper (`#f8f7f1`), blue rules every 36px, a double red margin. The whole homepage is handwritten in Caveat (400-700, one variable file): headings in black pen, notes and body in blue biro (`--play-biro`), numbers, eyebrows and corrections in red pen (`--play-red-pen`), links marked with highlighter (`--play-highlight`), never underlined. The H1 has a struck-out first draft (`.play-strike`, hidden from assistive tech). Bricolage and DM Sans are no longer used on the homepage; the skills pages keep DM Sans for long instructions and prompts and handwrite headings, titles and labels. JetBrains Mono stays inside the terminal placeholder, Permanent Marker on the suspended stamp.
- Writing sits on the rules. Every handwritten line is `--play-rule` (36px) tall, or a multiple via `round()`, and `src/scripts/notebook-rules.ts` (shared by the homepage and the skills layout) measures each written block's first baseline with a zero-size probe and nudges it onto the nearest rule with the `translate` property (no layout change; re-run on resize, font load and pin mode changes; the pinned stage is its own sheet). The kicker and swipe hint are no longer tilted. `output/playwright/brand-seo/ruled.cjs` checks first and last baselines at 320-1440 within 1.5px. The intro now says "mostly using Next.js, Node.js and Python", and the FTSE 100 line is not highlighted.
- The skills pages do the same for text written straight on the paper (topbar, breadcrumbs, kicker, headings, lede, notes, bullets, body, results line, footer; DM Sans body at a 36px line height). Text on white cards and yellow sheets is left alone, and so is anything inside a sticky element other than the paper (the desktop install column slides over the rules). `output/playwright/brand-seo/ruled-skills.cjs` checks the library and a skill page at 320-1440, top, middle and bottom, including that no nudged blocks overlap.
- The install counter Worker (`workers/skill-downloads`, deployed 2026-10-26 as version f8536d7f) accepts any `http://localhost` or `http://127.0.0.1` port as an origin as well as the live hosts, so the counter works under `astro preview` and the browser suites. `output/playwright/brand-seo/origin-check.mjs` checks the pattern rejects look-alikes; `node scripts/check-skill-downloads.mjs` checks production.
- Paper furniture: a static SVG grain tile in the `.play-world` background stack (5% opacity), hand-drawn two-stroke dividers, page numbers `p. 01`-`p. 03`, margin notes, a launch-ring caption and a handwritten "tryraisins" signature.
- Projects are screenshots taped into the book (`.play-print`: white border, two strips of tape, a per-tile `--play-tilt`) with handwritten notes beside or under them (`.play-copy`). On wide, tall screens they run as a scroll-pinned sequence (`reel.js`): only the print travels, and the notes stay on the page, rubbed out from the left in the first half of each turn and written in during the second half (clip-path from `--frame`), so two sets never overlap. In the static grid each print drops in and its notes are written in once. Below 901px the desk is a native scroll-snap carousel; `rail.js` adds a "3 of 7" counter, previous and next buttons, arrow/Home/End keys, a single tab stop on the print in view and polite announcements for user-initiated moves. Side prints shrink and turn via a scroll-driven animation where supported. Without JS the rail still swipes and every card stays tabbable. "Spread them out" / "Stack them up" still toggles the pinned mode.
- Prints are landscape (16:10), matching the 1440x900 covers, so no screenshot is cropped through its own headline. The Talent Hunter cover is a capture of its live landing page (the old one showed an authentication modal). `output/playwright/reel-perf/cover.cjs` re-captures it.
- The reel writes one custom property per print (`--frame`) from a single rAF-coalesced passive scroll listener; CSS derives transform and opacity. Geometry is measured on resize only. `data-reel-active` and `data-reel-progress` exist for tests. A print rests for 10% of its step, which keeps the sequence moving.
- Tops are now a hero toy: five above 1200px, four above 620px, three on phones. `spinning-tops.js` fades the fixed layer out (`data-idle`) once the hero falls below 12% visible and stops requesting frames entirely; it resumes when the hero returns. Physics and rendering are both paused, so the desk and contact sections cost nothing.
- Quality tiers, all at 60fps (never halve the rate; a 30fps cap reads as a stutter):
  - 0, modern desktop: device pixel ratio up to 1.5, multisampling, 1024 shadow map.
  - 1, phones and older laptops: ratio up to 1.25, multisampling, 512 shadow map.
  - 2, four threads or less (a 2013 MacBook): ratio 1, no multisampling, no shadow map.
  Measured on an Intel Iris 6100, the five tops hold 60fps at 1440x900 but drop frames at 1800x1125, which is why tier 2 renders at device pixels. A frame-time watchdog can drop one more tier at runtime. Clearcoat is gone (standard material) and the trail canvas renders at ratio 1.
- Doodles were the real bottleneck, not the tops. `doodle-notes.js` now samples at 1.7px (2.4 on lean machines), writes into flat coordinate arrays instead of allocating a point object per sample, measures each path once, batches strokes into three pressure buckets, draws at most one sketch per frame round-robin, and gives an expensive sketch its own lower rate instead of the page's. On the same machine the hero went from 50ms to 17ms per frame with the doodles running. Lean machines also skip the third pencil pass, coarser grain and the blend mode (`is-lean`).
- `public/og-notebook.png` was re-rendered from the new hero by `scripts/render-social-preview.js`. The card hides the tops and launch ring; its alt text describes the paper, handwriting and pencil sketches.
- First visit per tab opens with a paper sheet (`BootOverlay.astro` + `boot-overlay.ts`): the same ruled paper and red margin, a hand-drawn "tryraisins" signature drawn as SVG strokes, a pencil rule, and "laying out the page" beside a small spinning top. It holds until the first-screen faces load (Bricolage, DM Sans, and Caveat on the homepage; the mono and marker faces do not gate), with a 700ms floor, a 1400ms fonts cap and a 1600ms hard cap. Then a 200ms beat starts the hero entrance behind the still-covering sheet, followed by 620ms of travel with a slight tilt and shadow and removal at 820ms. `sessionStorage["tryraisins:boot"] = "seen"` skips it for the rest of the tab; a URL hash skips it, no-JS never sets `data-boot`, and reduced motion replaces the draw, spin and lift with a 150ms fade. State lives on `html[data-boot]`.
- The hero entrance runs on every load: header, kicker, both headline spans, intro and actions rise 520ms with a 60ms stagger. On a first visit the hero stays painted under the sheet so the headline is the LCP candidate; the hidden start is applied in the same task as the rise. Doodles and the three.js tops start on `boot:done`, after the sheet has left, so decorative work cannot block the first frame. Covers fade in 300ms after decode behind `data-img-fade`, so no-JS images stay visible.

## Decisions and Constraints

- Brand, not personal name: the site, titles, descriptions, Open Graph/Twitter tags, JSON-LD, manifest, social cards and signatures all say TryRaisins. The owner's first name appears exactly once, in the homepage hero kicker ("Hello, I'm Seun."), and never in metadata. The LinkedIn URL spells out the name, so it stays a visible link but is filtered out of the JSON-LD `sameAs`. `output/playwright/brand-seo/verify.cjs` checks this.
- No location on the site: Lagos, Nigeria, the time zone and `og:locale en_NG` were removed from the page, metadata, JSON-LD, manifest, `llms.txt` and the social cards. Terror Tracker's own description still mentions Nigeria because that is the project. The hero intro states that the work is used by multiple FTSE 100 companies across four continents and that TryRaisins maintains and contributes to open-source projects; the page description repeats it.
- The contact card is one heading, one line of copy and an availability line on the left, and the email button, its in-place reveal and the three profile links on the right. The "Contact sheet", "Write me an email" and "View profile" labels, the numbered profiles and the reserved empty slot are gone.
- `spinning-tops.js` loops now visit `liveCount()` tops (built tops only). Before, a phone-width landing on `#play-contact` threw `reading 'phase'` because the step loop ran before `prepare()` had built every top.
- Icons (`favicon.ico`, `favicon.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `logo192.png`, `logo512.png`) are rendered by `node scripts/render-icons.cjs`; the skills pages use the same self-hosted fonts as the homepage, with no Google Fonts request. `/llms.txt` is generated from the skills catalog.
- The pinned reel is progressive enhancement: the static grid is the source of truth, and pinning only ever adds `data-reel-mode="pinned"`.
- The loading sheet may never be why content stays hidden: the inline bootstrap has a 4 second failsafe, the lift is capped at 1600ms from first paint, and `whenBootRevealed` also resolves on a rejected boot. Keep all three.
- Keep the first-visit hero painted under the sheet. Do not set `data-hero-enter` before the reveal on that path; it cost about 1.3s of throttled LCP. The hidden start belongs to the repeat path only, and the module sets it in the same task as `data-enter`.
- Do not gate the lift on cover decoding. Covers have their own `data-img-fade` reveal after `decode()`.
- The sheet shows once per tab session and never on a URL with a hash. No-JS must leave `data-boot` unset and every hero line and cover visible.
- Keep the 200ms hold and the hero entrance in step with the 620ms peel (`TIMING.hold` and the CSS transition delay in `BootOverlay.astro`). Starting both at 0ms left an empty page behind the lifting sheet; starting the entrance after the lift made it pop. Measured at the moment the sheet's bottom edge reaches the headline, the first line is 72-84% and the second 41-60%.
- Doodles and three.js mount on `boot:done`, never during the peel. Mounting them at reveal blocked the first frame of the entrance for about 160ms and left the page blank under the sheet edge.
- The sticky sheet paints its own paper so the paused tops layer cannot show through it.
- Never cap the tops or doodles below 60fps to save work. Reduce resolution, shadows or per-frame work instead; a halved rate is what reads as a stutter. The doodles, not the tops, are the expensive part: keep one sketch per frame and the flat-array renderer.
- Do not reintroduce the shuffle, Barlow Condensed, Public Sans, a Google Fonts link, `mix-blend-mode` on more than one doodle layer per view, or more than five tops on desktop.
- Keep the notebook rules, the red margin rule, readable content clear of the rule, 44px targets and the 3px blue focus ring.
- Writing style: natural prose, no em dashes, no staccato fragments, no decorative underlines on links.
- Preserve unrelated `.playwright-cli/` user state. Never commit browser scripts, screenshots or generated artifacts; `output/` is ignored.
