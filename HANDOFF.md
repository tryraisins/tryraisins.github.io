Sketch parts animate after drawing: blinking faces, a wagging cat tail, walking dinosaur, swaying flower, waving robot, swimming fish, turning wheels and other object-specific movement.
- The lifecycle remains 5.2 seconds drawing, 11 seconds alive, 2.6 seconds erasing, with randomized 1-3 second initial gaps. Completed-cycle counting advances sketches even when a frame skips the erase boundary. Rendering uses device pixel ratio up to 2, runs at 30 FPS, skips offscreen drawings and pauses hidden-tab clocks. Reduced motion displays completed static originals.
- Spinning tops (Option B, approved 2026-10-12: they roam the whole fixed viewport layer). Eight tops above 1200px, four above 620px, three on phones, at radius 56/46/34. Five silhouettes (turned wood, lithographed tin with clearcoat, steel battle blades, machined brass gear, turbine); the 6th-8th tops repeat wood/tin/blades in green, yellow and purple. Tops are built only when the width needs them.
- Lifecycle in `spinning-tops.js`: `airborne` (staggered drop with tip bounce) → `spinning` (sleep) → `precessing` (rate ≈ 105/ω, tilt grows below ω 30) → `wobbling` (nutation, tip walks in loops) → `rimroll` (precession accelerates as it flattens) → `resting` (rocks, then still). Spin decay is tip friction + air drag ∝ ω + wobble scrub, randomised per top, so free spins last roughly 30–38 s and tops fall at different times. New round 3 s after all rest.
- Collisions (made livelier 2026-10-12): restitution ~0.82-0.92 plus a spin-driven `rimKick` that flings rims apart (harder for same-direction spins); same-direction hits lose 3-8% spin; opposite directions hand spin from the faster to the slower top and can wake a resting one; hits add a hop (up to 170) and nod, with a much smaller tilt kick when a hop lands than when a top is first dropped; only a hit with kick > 150 on a wobbling top below spin 14 can knock it over (made harder 2026-10-12: first falls moved from ~7 s to 14-20 s). Lively tops (spin > 22) cruise at ~120 px/s (scaled by radius) and steer toward the nearest other spinning top, so a round produces ~30 hits in 20 s. Walls bounce at 0.78. Sparks only metal on metal (and mouse on metal tops); wood/tin leave a graphite scuff.
- Rendering: blades/teeth/fins/studs are baked as transparent "detail" batches that fade into streaked blur rings as spin rises, while the body turns at a capped display rate (no strobing). Contact shadow under the tip spreads as the top leans. Pencil trails go on a 2D canvas under WebGL, batched once per rendered frame and faded in 0.6 s steps (~8 s to vanish). Keep trail drawing out of the physics step: stroking per step cost 0.84 ms/step vs 0.011 ms without.
- Launch ring: no drag-to-launch and no Launch button (removed 2026-10-12). `[data-launch-ring]` is an empty SVG hidden by CSS; `launch-ring.js` (`createLaunchRing`) fills it and is passed to `mountSpinningTops` as `ring`. Nothing is drawn until the tops are ready. Each round `reset()` calls `ring.draw()`, which sketches a new random child-like loop (lumpy radius, drift so it misses its start, overshoot, partial retrace, feTurbulence grain) with uneven speed over ~1.1-1.35 s plus a ~0.45 s retrace, and returns its duration; the first top drops 0.2 s before it finishes and the rest follow every 0.32 s. Tops land spread round the ring and are flung outward at 150-260 px/s. Once every top has landed and left the ring ellipse at least once (`leftRing` flag, since tops chasing each other cross back in), `setRingClear(true)` sets `data-ring-clear` on the pad and `data-ring="clear"` on the battlefield, fades the note and calls `ring.erase()`: a masked back-and-forth eraser stroke travels round the loop over ~1.5 s and leaves a faint smudge that fades, then the SVG is hidden. Reduced motion shows the complete ring without animation, still tops and no trails. Filmstrip: `node output/playwright/spin-redesign/ring-filmstrip.cjs`.
- Loading (2026-10-12): `Play.astro` dynamic-imports `spinning-tops.js` at the first idle callback, so three.js (151 KB gz) is its own chunk and no longer blocks the page script. The module renders nothing until `prepare()` finishes: it compiles every shader with `renderer.compileAsync` (KHR_parallel_shader_compile) — RoomEnvironment, PMREM blur/GGX (via private `_setSize/_allocateTargets`, guarded), a MeshDepthMaterial shadow probe, and all top materials against a CubeUV stand-in texture of the bake's size — builds one top per frame meanwhile, then bakes a 64px environment and sets `data-ready`. `renderer.debug.checkShaderErrors` is off and the shadow type is `PCFShadowMap` because r186 silently renders PCFSoft as PCF, which made every precompiled program miss. At 4x CPU throttle the longest main-thread task fell from 1.0-2.3 s to 0.2-0.5 s and page scripts run ~0.8 s sooner; unthrottled, tops start ~0.5 s after network idle. Measure with `node output/playwright/spin-load/load.cjs http://127.0.0.1:4322/ <label>` (reports in that folder; `report-before.json` is the old build).
- `?spin-debug` exposes `window.__spinTops.advance(seconds)`, `.tops()` and `.size()` for fast-forward tests; wait for `[data-battlefield][data-ready]` first. Datasets: `ready`, `ring`, `phases`, `spinRange`, `topState`, `hitCount`, `scuffs`, `simTime`.
