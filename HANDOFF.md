## Current Objective and State

Pocket Playground is the production TryRaisins portfolio. `src/pages/index.astro` imports `src/prototypes/portfolio-redesign/Play.astro`. The page is a ruled notebook on a crumpled sheet with pencil drawings, and it reads as a physical sketchbook: Caveat handwriting for annotations and a contents page of taped project prints. The spinning tops, their three.js scene, the launch ring and the loading-sheet top were removed on 2026-10-28 and `three` is no longer a dependency.

- Copy, project data, destinations, the suspended BetPicks treatment, the contact reveal, the XOR-masked email and all SEO/JSON-LD are unchanged. Preserve them.
- Fonts are self-hosted from `src/styles/fonts.css` + `public/fonts/*.woff2` (five families, latin only): Bricolage Grotesque 600, DM Sans 400-600, Caveat 400-700, JetBrains Mono 400, Permanent Marker 400. There is no Google Fonts request. Barlow Condensed and Public Sans were removed; do not reintroduce them. The handwritten stacks (`--play-hand`, `--sk-hand` and the boot caption) fall through `'Segoe Print', 'Chalkboard SE', 'Bradley Hand'` to system sans and never end in the generic `cursive` keyword: a browser that blocks webfonts, which some in-app browsers do, resolves `cursive` to Snell Roundhand on iOS or Dancing Script on Android, and that reads as an unreadable script on a phone. Keep the readable tail.
- The page is a school exercise book: off-white paper (`#f8f7f1`), blue rules every 36px, a double red margin. The whole homepage is handwritten in Caveat (400-700, one variable file): headings in black pen, notes and body in blue biro (`--play-biro`), numbers, eyebrows and corrections in red pen (`--play-red-pen`), links marked with highlighter (`--play-highlight`), never underlined. The H1 has a struck-out first draft (`.play-strike`, hidden from assistive tech). Bricolage and DM Sans are no longer used on the homepage; the skills pages keep DM Sans for long instructions and prompts and handwrite headings, titles and labels. JetBrains Mono stays inside the terminal placeholder, Permanent Marker on the suspended stamp.
- Writing sits on the rules. Every handwritten line is `--play-rule` (36px) tall, or a multiple via `round()`, and `src/scripts/notebook-rules.ts` (shared by the homepage and the skills layout) measures each written block's first baseline with a zero-size probe and nudges it onto the nearest rule with the `translate` property (no layout change; re-run on resize, font load and pin mode changes; the pinned stage is its own sheet). The kicker and swipe hint are no longer tilted. `output/playwright/brand-seo/ruled.cjs` checks first and last baselines at 320-1440 within 1.5px. The intro now says "mostly using Next.js, Node.js and Python", and the FTSE 100 line is not highlighted.
- The skills pages do the same for text written straight on the paper (topbar, breadcrumbs, kicker, headings, lede, notes, bullets, body, results line, footer; DM Sans body at a 36px line height). Text on white cards and yellow sheets is left alone, and so is anything inside a sticky element other than the paper (the desktop install column slides over the rules). `output/playwright/brand-seo/ruled-skills.cjs` checks the library and a skill page at 320-1440, top, middle and bottom, including that no nudged blocks overlap.
- The install counter Worker (`workers/skill-downloads`, deployed 2026-10-26 as version 2a940db9) accepts any `http://localhost` or `http://127.0.0.1` port as an origin as well as the live hosts, so the counter works under `astro preview` and the browser suites. It returns `{ total, updated, changed }`: `changed` is when the number last moved and `changed_at` in `download_total` holds it, because npm batches its daily figures and the two drift apart. npm also leaves whole days out of its feed (6, 13-15 and 19-21 October 2026 reported 0 for react too), so the card can sit still for days with nothing wrong. npm's relative endpoints (`last-day`, `last-week`, `last-month`) are stuck in June 2026 for yeknal, so npmjs.com shows 23 weekly downloads while the range endpoint has 8,040. The card shows `Updated <date>` only once the figure has been still for 36 hours, lands the final number on a timer when rAF is throttled, and re-reads on `pageshow` and `visibilitychange`. `output/playwright/brand-seo/origin-check.mjs` checks the origin pattern rejects look-alikes; `node output/playwright/skill-tally/tally.cjs http://localhost:4330` covers the card states; `node scripts/check-skill-downloads.mjs` checks production and fails when the total has not been recomputed in a day or has not moved in a week.
- Paper furniture: a static SVG grain tile in the `.play-world` background stack (5% opacity), hand-drawn two-stroke dividers, margin notes and a handwritten "tryraisins" signature. The homepage sheet is crumpled, as if it had spent a while in a pocket: `--play-crumple` and `--play-crumple-fine` (feTurbulence lit by feDiffuseLighting, converted to dark and light alpha, then faded by a low-frequency noise mask so the crumpling comes and goes) sit above the grain and rules in `.play-world` only, as static tiles of different sizes and seeds with no blend mode. Fold lines were tried and removed at the owner's request, so do not add them back. The rules stay straight so `notebook-rules.ts` still lands writing on them. The skills pages and the loading sheet keep the flat paper.
- Projects are a contents page. Each `<li>` in `[data-play-grid]` holds an entry link (number, name, type, year) and a panel with the taped print (`.play-print`: white border, two strips of tape, a per-tile `--play-tilt`), the description and a highlighted "View project" label. On wide screens with a fine pointer every item is a full-size subgrid, so the entries stack down the left and all prints share one cell on the right; only the `[data-current]` item's panel is visible, and items pass pointer events through to their entry and print. `contents.js` moves `data-current` on focus and after a 90ms pointer rest (so a diagonal move to the print does not swap it), and adds ArrowUp/ArrowDown/Home/End between entries, skipping the suspended one. The section is one screen tall; nothing is scroll-pinned. Narrower or touch screens get the items as cards (two columns, one at 620px and below) with the print first, and each print drops in once. The print and label links are `tabindex="-1"` and `aria-hidden`, so the entry is the single tab stop and its `aria-describedby` points at the description. Without JS the first print shows and every entry is still a link.
- Prints are landscape (16:10), matching the 1440x900 covers, so no screenshot is cropped through its own headline. The Dyaad cover is a capture of the old Talent Hunter landing page and still shows that name; re-capture it once `dyaad.tryraisins.dev` resolves. `output/playwright/reel-perf/cover.cjs` re-captures it.
- The reel writes one custom property per print (`--frame`) from a single rAF-coalesced passive scroll listener; CSS derives transform and opacity. Geometry is measured on resize only. `data-reel-active` and `data-reel-progress` exist for tests. A print rests for 10% of its step, which keeps the sequence moving.
- Doodles were the real bottleneck, not the tops. `doodle-notes.js` now samples at 1.7px (2.4 on lean machines), writes into flat coordinate arrays instead of allocating a point object per sample, measures each path once, batches strokes into three pressure buckets, draws at most one sketch per frame round-robin, and gives an expensive sketch its own lower rate instead of the page's. On the same machine the hero went from 50ms to 17ms per frame with the doodles running. Lean machines also skip the third pencil pass, coarser grain and the blend mode (`is-lean`).
- `public/og-notebook.png` was rendered from the hero by `scripts/render-social-preview.js` (it predates the crumpled paper; re-run the script to refresh it). Its alt text describes the paper, handwriting and pencil sketches.
- First visit per tab opens with a paper sheet (`BootOverlay.astro` + `boot-overlay.ts`): the same ruled paper and red margin, a hand-drawn "tryraisins" signature drawn as SVG strokes, a pencil rule, and "laying out the page". It holds until the first-screen faces load (Caveat on the homepage, plus DM Sans on the skills pages; the mono and marker faces do not gate), with a 700ms floor, a 1400ms fonts cap and a 1600ms hard cap. Then a 200ms beat starts the hero entrance behind the still-covering sheet, followed by 620ms of travel with a slight tilt and shadow and removal at 820ms. `sessionStorage["tryraisins:boot"] = "seen"` skips it for the rest of the tab; a URL hash skips it, no-JS never sets `data-boot`, and reduced motion replaces the draw and lift with a 150ms fade. State lives on `html[data-boot]`.
- The hero entrance runs on every load: header, kicker, both headline spans, intro and actions rise 520ms with a 60ms stagger. On a first visit the hero stays painted under the sheet so the headline is the LCP candidate; the hidden start is applied in the same task as the rise. Doodles start on `boot:done`, after the sheet has left, so decorative work cannot block the first frame. Covers fade in 300ms after decode behind `data-img-fade`, so no-JS images stay visible.

## Decisions and Constraints

- Brand, not personal name: the site, titles, descriptions, Open Graph/Twitter tags, JSON-LD, manifest, social cards and signatures all say TryRaisins. The owner's first name appears exactly once, in the homepage hero kicker ("Hello, I'm Seun."), and never in metadata. The LinkedIn URL spells out the name, so it stays a visible link but is filtered out of the JSON-LD `sameAs`. `output/playwright/brand-seo/verify.cjs` checks this.
- No location on the site: Lagos, Nigeria, the time zone and `og:locale en_NG` were removed from the page, metadata, JSON-LD, manifest, `llms.txt` and the social cards. Terror Tracker's own description still mentions Nigeria because that is the project. The hero intro states that the work is used by multiple FTSE 100 companies across four continents and that TryRaisins maintains and contributes to open-source projects; the page description repeats it.
- The contact card is an airmail postcard, taped on and slightly crooked: a red and blue striped border, the heading and one line of copy on the left, and on the right a perforated stamp that holds the availability note (text only, no status dot) with a postmark across its top that is struck again every 7 seconds (the seal turns once while the three lines ripple; still under reduced motion), then the address side (the email button and its in-place reveal, the profile links and the signature) beside a thin centre rule. Below 981px it stacks and the stamp sits top right above the heading. The XOR-masked email and the reveal in `src/scripts/contact-reveal.ts` are unchanged. The profiles are GitHub and LinkedIn only: Medium was removed from the page, `src/data/profile.ts` (and so llms.txt) and the JSON-LD `sameAs` at the owner's request. Pages are no longer labelled `p. 01`-`p. 03`.
- Icons (`favicon.ico`, `favicon.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `logo192.png`, `logo512.png`) are rendered by `node scripts/render-icons.cjs`; the skills pages use the same self-hosted fonts as the homepage, with no Google Fonts request. `/llms.txt` is generated from the skills catalog.
- The pinned reel is progressive enhancement: the static grid is the source of truth, and pinning only ever adds `data-reel-mode="pinned"`.
- The loading sheet may never be why content stays hidden: the inline bootstrap has a 4 second failsafe, the lift is capped at 1600ms from first paint, and `whenBootRevealed` also resolves on a rejected boot. Keep all three.
- Keep the first-visit hero painted under the sheet. Do not set `data-hero-enter` before the reveal on that path; it cost about 1.3s of throttled LCP. The hidden start belongs to the repeat path only, and the module sets it in the same task as `data-enter`.
- Do not gate the lift on cover decoding. Covers have their own `data-img-fade` reveal after `decode()`.
- The sheet shows once per tab session and never on a URL with a hash. No-JS must leave `data-boot` unset and every hero line and cover visible.
- Keep the 200ms hold and the hero entrance in step with the 620ms peel (`TIMING.hold` and the CSS transition delay in `BootOverlay.astro`). Starting both at 0ms left an empty page behind the lifting sheet; starting the entrance after the lift made it pop. Measured at the moment the sheet's bottom edge reaches the headline, the first line is 72-84% and the second 41-60%.
- Doodles mount on `boot:done`, never during the peel. Mounting them at reveal blocked the first frame of the entrance for about 160ms and left the page blank under the sheet edge.
- Never cap the doodles below 60fps to save work. Reduce resolution or per-frame work instead; a halved rate is what reads as a stutter. The doodles are the expensive part of the hero: keep one sketch per frame and the flat-array renderer.
- Do not reintroduce the shuffle, Barlow Condensed, Public Sans, a Google Fonts link, `mix-blend-mode` on more than one doodle layer per view, or the spinning tops and three.js (removed on request).
- Keep the notebook rules, the red margin rule, readable content clear of the rule, 44px targets and the 3px blue focus ring.
- Writing style: natural prose, no em dashes, no staccato fragments, no decorative underlines on links.
- Preserve unrelated `.playwright-cli/` user state. Never commit browser scripts, screenshots or generated artifacts; `output/` is ignored.
