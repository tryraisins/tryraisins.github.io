When to use it

  • You need narration for a video or demo.
  • You want an audio version of written content.
  • Several clips should share the same voice.

Creating custom voices is out of scope.

A practical example

You ask

“Record a calm voiceover for this 60-second product script.”

Your agent does

It picks a suitable voice, generates the audio and saves it ready to drop into your edit.
