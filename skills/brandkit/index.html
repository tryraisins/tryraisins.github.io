When to use it

  • You want visual concepts for a new brand or product identity.
  • You need a brand-guidelines board to share with a team.
  • You are exploring logo directions before committing to one.

A practical example

You ask

“Create a brand board for a calm budgeting app: logo ideas, palette and type.”

Your agent does

It plans the identity, then generates a board with logo concepts, colours, type and a couple of mockups.
