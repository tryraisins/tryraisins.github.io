When to use it

  • You need product shots, covers or concept art.
  • An image needs inpainting or its background replaced.
  • You want several variants generated in one go.

A practical example

You ask

“Make three cover images for the blog post with a transparent background.”

Your agent does

It writes the prompts, runs the bundled script and saves the variants for you to choose from.

What gets installed

The whole folder is copied, so scripts, references and any license notices stay together.
