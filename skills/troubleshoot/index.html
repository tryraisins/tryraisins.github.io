When to use it

  • Something broke and you don’t know why.
  • A build or deployment fails with an unclear error.
  • Performance got worse after a change.

A practical example

You ask

“The app crashes when I open the settings page. Figure out why.”

Your agent does

It reproduces the crash, traces it to the cause, fixes it and confirms the page opens.
