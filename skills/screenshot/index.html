Screenshots

Captures desktop or system screenshots of the whole screen, one window or a region, for when the browser or tool can’t capture what you need.

When to use it

  • You explicitly want a screenshot of the desktop or an app window.
  • Tool-specific capture isn’t available.
  • You need a precise region of the screen.

A practical example

You ask

“Take a screenshot of the simulator window.”

Your agent does

It finds the window, captures it and saves the image where you can open it.

What gets installed

The whole folder is copied, so scripts, references and any license notices stay together.
