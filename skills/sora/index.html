When to use it

  • You want a short AI-generated video.
  • An existing Sora clip needs editing or extending.
  • You are planning a batch of related videos.

A practical example

You ask

“Generate a five-second loop of coffee being poured for the landing page.”

Your agent does

It writes the prompt, submits the job, waits for it and downloads the video and thumbnail.
