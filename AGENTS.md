# Portfolio Writing Style

- Write user-facing prose in a natural, conversational flow with varied sentence lengths.
- Avoid staccato sequences, including several short standalone sentences that split one connected thought into punchy fragments.
- Join related ideas when that makes the meaning clearer, and keep headings, buttons, labels, and navigation concise when they are functioning as interface text.
- Use clear text labels for links without decorative arrows, text underlines, or bottom-only borders. Preserve keyboard focus outlines, readable contrast, and generous click targets; use color, background, or a complete border for link affordance.
- Keep copy specific to the work and avoid generic slogans, artificial reassurance, and repeated decorative text underlines. Preserve the notebook rules and structural dividers.
